01 / SIGN IN

Connect your wallet

Use the same wallet to return to your account and saved companies.

  1. Choose Connect wallet on the home page.
  2. Select Sign in with wallet and choose the account in your wallet.
  3. Review and sign the verification message.
  4. After verification, the application returns to your requested page.
Opening Saved companies while signed out shows the sign-in page. Admin access also requires an authorized admin wallet.

A sign-in signature verifies your identity. Review any separate transaction request and its purpose before proceeding.

02 / EXPLORE

Explore and save companies

Open Market → Explore, or choose Explore platform on the home page.

  1. Choose a Country to filter the available results. If an Update country button appears, select it to apply the choice.
  2. Read the company card: rank, symbol, name, price, and the displayed indicators.
  3. Select View Details to open the linked TradingView page in a new tab.
  4. After signing in, use the heart to save a company. Return through Market → Saved companies.
Your access determines which ranks and how many companies you can view. Results do not necessarily start at rank 1. Use Saved companies to organize your saved items.

02 / STOCKS IN DETAIL

Read a stock card

Check the company identity before opening more details.

ItemHow to read it
RankThe position displayed by EPSX using its ranking methodology.
Symbol / nameThe large ticker and the company name below it identify the company. Check both when symbols look similar.
PriceThe value supplied to the card when the page loads. Check the displayed currency. The card does not show a last-price timestamp.
Heart / View DetailsThe heart saves a company. View Details links to TradingView in a new tab.
For further chart viewing, select View Details and confirm the company and exchange on the destination page. The current link includes the ticker without an exchange prefix.

02 / STOCKS IN DETAIL

Open details and save stocks

Use the actions on the company card to continue.

Open further details

  1. Check the symbol and company name on the card.
  2. Select View Details. The link is configured to open TradingView in a new tab. Confirm the company and exchange there.
  3. Return to the EPSX tab to compare another company.

Keep a company for later

Select the heart. If signed out, you will see the sign-in page shown above. After signing in, check whether the company is saved and save it if necessary. Open Market → Saved companies to revisit saved items.

02 / STOCKS IN DETAIL

Filter by country

Choose a market and inspect the refreshed results.

  1. Open Market → Explore and select the Country field.
  2. Choose Thailand or another country. The ready interface updates automatically; use Update country if that button is shown.
  3. Check that the selected country and company cards change. Changing the country starts at page 1.
  4. Choose All Countries to return to the combined results.
Some countries have no results within your available rank range. This is different from a failed request. Review the access note below the results or select another country.

02 / STOCKS IN DETAIL

Pages and accessible ranks

Scroll below the cards to inspect pagination and your access range.

  1. Showing indicates the visible item positions and total available results. The numbers may change.
  2. Use Next / Prev or a page number to move through the results.
  3. Select Per page to adjust page size. The captured options are 10, 25, 50, and 100. Use Apply page size if shown.
  4. Check Viewing and any locked-rank note. Choose Review plans to compare access options.
Showing positions and Rank are different. The first visible item can be rank 100 even when Showing starts at 1. Changing pages does not unlock ranks outside your access.

03 / PLANS

Compare plans and access

Open Market → Plans before starting a purchase.

  1. Compare the plan name, price, duration, and listed features.
  2. Read the Company rankings line for its rank range and result allowance. Check API access if needed.
  3. Select Review plan, then review the checkout details.
  4. After completing a purchase, check Account → Purchases and Account → Access.
This screenshot explains the layout. Prices, promotions, and terms may change. If access does not appear after payment, check the existing order before trying again.

05 / ADMIN

Start with Admin

Open the Admin site and use a wallet with administrator access.

  1. Select a wallet on the Admin sign-in page.
  2. Check the account and verification message before signing.
  3. After authorization, open the menus available to your role.

Account administration

Wallet management contains wallet accounts, access, plans, and credits. Select the target account before reviewing details.

Platform administration

Routes include payments, Pay escrows, news, notifications, chat, developer tools, and audit logs, subject to access.

06 / EPSX PAY

Meet EPSX Pay

A merchant workspace for payment links and hosted checkout.

  1. Choose Start accepting payments or Dashboard.
  2. Connect MetaMask, sign in, and follow the shop-name setup.
  3. Create a package or payment link and share its checkout.
  4. Track the outcome in Payments and review collection into the merchant wallet.
The checkout preview on the home page is labeled Sample only. It is not evidence of an actual payment. Keep Test and Live environments separate.

A shop is tied to its owner wallet and receiving wallet. Choose the intended wallet when starting.

08 / QUICK HELP

Troubleshooting and scope

Check the following before contacting support.

IssueWhat to check
Wallet will not connectOpen and unlock your wallet, check the selected account, and try sign-in again. Cancelling the signature leaves you signed out.
Returned to sign-inSign in again with the same wallet and reopen the requested menu.
Admin access deniedCheck that the wallet has admin permissions. Connecting alone does not grant them.
No country resultsCheck Country and the available rank range. An empty accessible result is different from a service error. Try another country or clear the filter.
Payment not updatedCheck Purchases or Payments and the selected environment. Keep the order reference and transaction hash for support.
